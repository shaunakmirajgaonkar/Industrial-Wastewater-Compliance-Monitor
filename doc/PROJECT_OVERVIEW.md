# Project Overview

Industrial Wastewater Compliance Monitor is a 100% local-first environmental
governance and operational screening dashboard.

It analyzes locally supplied flow, chemical, treatment, weather, inspection,
corrective-action and facility signals to identify records that may warrant
additional compliance review.

## Main capabilities

- Explainable 0–100 screening risk score
- LOW / MODERATE / HIGH / CRITICAL classification
- Chemical parameter screening
- Flow and discharge-load analytics
- Treatment-performance monitoring
- Weather impact analysis
- Inspection intelligence
- Compliance control tower
- Facility intelligence
- Parameter analytics
- Compliance timeline
- What-if scenario analysis
- Rule-based alerts
- CSV upload, validation and export
